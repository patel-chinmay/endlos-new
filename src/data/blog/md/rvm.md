## How Does a Reverse Vending Machine Work?
  <p>Operating an RVM is simple and intuitive. Here’s a step-by-step guide:</p>

#### Step 1 - Deposit the Recyclable Item

<p>The user places an empty bottle or can into the designated input slot of the machine.</p>

#### Step 2 - Identification and Sorting

<p>The RVM utilizes advanced recognition technology to scan the item, verifying details like size, material, and barcode to ensure it qualifies for recycling.</p>

#### Step 3 - Compaction and Storage

<p>Once validated, the machine either crushes the container to optimize storage space or directs it into a designated compartment for collection. Machines equipped with specialized reverse vending machine parts, such as shredders and compactors, enhance efficiency by processing large volumes of recyclables.</p>

#### Step 4 - Issuing Rewards

<p>To encourage continued participation, the RVM provides an incentive—whether in the form of cashback, discount vouchers, or digital reward points.</p>

#### Step 5 - Collection and Recycling

<p>When the storage unit reaches capacity, recyclables are retrieved and transported to processing facilities, where they are transformed into new products.</p>
